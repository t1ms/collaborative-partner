# Well-Formed Outcomes (WFO)

Source: paper §5. An amorphous complaint cannot be resolved until converted into a Well-Formed Outcome. Five non-negotiable predicates; each has a detection trigger, an intervention question, and a pass metric — i.e., a testable exit condition for the agent.

## The 5 predicates

1. **Positively Formulated** — goal must be presence, not absence (dY/dt > 0). "Stop procrastinating" neurologically compels simulating procrastination.
   - Detect: negation operators (stop, avoid, quit, don't want, free from).
   - Ask: "If you were completely free from [X], what specific behavior or state will replace it immediately?"

2. **Self-Initiated & Controlled** — domain of action ⊆ user's capabilities.
   - Detect: external reference (my boss, the market, my spouse, luck).
   - Ask: "What part of this outcome depends exclusively on your direct physical and mental actions?"

3. **Sensory-Grounded Verification** — S* = {observable visual, auditory, kinesthetic cues}.
   - Detect: abstract nominalizations (peace, clarity, success, freedom).
   - Ask: "What exact visual cue, auditory feedback, or physical metric will prove it to a neutral observer with a video camera?"

4. **Ecological / Systemic Integrity** — change preserves core values; no catastrophic side-effects.
   - Detect: blind enthusiasm, uncalculated trade-offs, internal hesitation/resistance.
   - Ask: "What does keeping this problem currently protect you from? What will you have to surrender once it's solved?"
   - **Positive-Intent Ladder (Core Transformation backward chaining):** If the user exhibits internal conflict or resistance ("I want to delegate, but I can't let go of control"), chain backward to the underlying protective goal:
     - Step 1: "When you hold onto control, what higher protective outcome is that trying to achieve?" (e.g., "Prevent product errors")
     - Step 2: "And if errors were prevented, what higher value does that secure for you/the company?" (e.g., "Reputation and safety")
     - Step 3: Re-integrate into WFO: "How do we design the delegation outcome so that reputation and safety remain 100% protected?"

5. **Granularity / Chunk Size** — action scale matches cognitive bandwidth.
   - If paralyzed → down-chunk: "What is the very first 3-minute physical action?"
   - If unmotivated → up-chunk: "For what higher strategic purpose does this step exist?"

## Canonical walkthrough (imposter syndrome → WFO)

User: "I want to stop feeling like an imposter leading exec meetings."
1. Positive: "What state instead?" → "confident, authoritative."
2. Sensory: "Confidence is abstract — on video, what would show it?" → hands calm on table, measured pace, no fillers, 3–4s eye contact after key points.
3. Locus: "Can you do those regardless of whether they agree?" → yes, physical choices.
4. Ecology: "What does the imposter feeling protect you from?" → lowered expectations, a ready excuse. "Willing to own full accountability?" → yes.
5. Chunk: "First 90 seconds of tomorrow's standup — exact opening sentence, grounded posture?"
