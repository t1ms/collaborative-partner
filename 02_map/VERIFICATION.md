## 2. Technical fidelity — VERIFIED with two notes

- **Meta-Model 11-pattern matrix** ✅ — matches canonical Structure of Magic set (canonical lists run 11–12). The one canonical pattern the paper's matrix underplays is **Presuppositions** (nominalization is covered separately in §1, which is a defensible organizational choice). Precision-question forms match standard practice.
- **Well-Formed Outcome 5 predicates** ✅ — positive/self-initiated/sensory-evidence/ecology are the canonical core 4. **Chunking/granularity is a legitimate but less universal addition** — appears in many practitioner lists (Dilts) but isn't in the minimal canonical set. No fabrication.
- **Perceptual Positions** ⚠️ — 1st/2nd/3rd canonical (Grinder & DeLozier 1987). **4th "systemic" position is Dilts' addition (Meta-Mirror, 1988–92), not Bandler's.** Paper presents all four as Bandler's framework.
- **Swish, 6-step reframing, content/context reframing, positive intent** ✅ — match canonical descriptions. (Six-step reframing itself was codified by Leslie Cameron-Bandler in 1978 out of B&G's work — a nuance, not an error.)
- **TOTE "three structural pathologies"** — the pathologies framing is the **paper's own synthesis**, not from Miller et al. Label as novel synthesis if cited externally.

## 3. Empirical status — the evidence base & defensible posture

- **Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses (Witkowski 2010; Passmore & Rowson 2018; Sturt et al. 2012)**: Over 70% of empirical trials refute the foundational tenets of classic NLP (specifically Preferred Representational Systems / predicate matching, and Eye Accessing Cues as deception markers). Classical computational software metaphors ("reprogramming internal neurology") are scientifically invalid.
- **Validated Derivatives & Active Ingredients (Muss 1991; Gray & Bourke 2017; Astill Wright et al. 2023, RETURN Trial)**: Clinical offshoots (Visual-Kinesthetic Dissociation / Rewind, RTM Protocol, Core Transformation) demonstrate empirical support via established neurobiological mechanisms: **prediction-error-driven memory reconsolidation**, **psychological distancing** (3rd-person observer position downregulating sympathetic arousal), and **cognitive restructuring**.
- **Verdict**: Classic NLP as a grand theory is unsupported and pseudoscientific. Derivative cognitive tools, when decoupled from pseudoscientific metatheory, function effectively through standard cognitive and clinical neuroscience mechanisms.

**Defensible positioning (non-negotiable):** 
1. Pitch the system strictly as a **structured problem-clarification engine** ("a formal grammar of problem statements with a deterministic Socratic question-routing engine").
2. Explicitly anchor to established psychological lineages:
   - **Meta-Model** ≈ Socratic inquiry + CBT/ACT cognitive distortion restructuring.
   - **Well-Formed Outcomes** ≈ Goal-setting literature (Locke & Latham) + Motivational Interviewing.
   - **Perceptual Positions** ≈ Cognitive perspective-taking & psychological distancing.
   - **Clean Language** ≈ David Grove's zero-metaphor contamination protocols.
   - **Positive-Intent Laddering** ≈ Core Transformation's values-based backward chaining.
3. Completely exclude disconfirmed constructs: NO sensory predicate matching (PRS), NO eye-accessing cues (EAC), NO hypnotherapy trance inductions.
4. Flag rhetorical mathematics: equations and derivative notation (`∂S/∂θ`, `dim(S)`) are purely metaphoric design shorthand, not executable computations.

## Net assessment

The paper is **bibliographically honest and technically faithful** to the source corpus. The derivative empirical literature reinforces our design choices: prune the pseudoscientific cargo-cult elements (PRS, EAC), retain the high-leverage cognitive restructuring tools (Meta-Model, WFO, Clean Language), and ground the user-facing experience in transparent first-principles Socratic debugging.
