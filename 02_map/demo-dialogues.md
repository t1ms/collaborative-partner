# Demo Dialogues — Ready-Made Test Transcripts

Source: research §2.2 and §5.3. All examples are grounded in professional and decision territory (executive communication, product strategy, technical leadership). Use these as evaluation cases and test scripts.

## Meta-Model interventions (§2.2)

1. **Simple deletion** — "I just feel completely overwhelmed by this project."
   → "Overwhelmed by which specific component? If you pointed at the single task or variable causing the most friction, what are you looking at?"
2. **Comparative deletion** — "We have to build a much faster architecture."
   → "Faster than what baseline? What's the exact current latency, and the precise threshold for 'fast enough'?"
3. **Unspecified referent** — "They are never going to approve this budget."
   → "Who specifically on the committee is 'they'? Which individual holds veto power?"
4. **Unspecified verb** — "My co-founder is completely undermining my authority."
   → "How specifically? What exact words or physical actions in your last meeting did you classify as undermining?"
5. **Cause-effect** — "His dismissive tone makes me shut down in meetings."
   → "How specifically does his vocal pitch compel your silence? If you kept speaking through that tone, what feeling would you have to tolerate?"
6. **Mind reading** — "My team thinks I'm incompetent as technical lead."
   → "What observable data told you that — exact words, or a facial expression you're interpreting as a monologue?"
7. **Complex equivalence** — "She didn't invite me to the planning dinner, which means she's pushing me out."
   → "How does one missing dinner reservation equal a systemic termination campaign? Ever forgotten to invite someone you fully valued?"
8. **Lost performative** — "It's unprofessional to show frustration with clients."
   → "Who established that rule? In what context would authentic urgency be the highest professional service to a client?"
9. **Universal quantifier** — "Every time I try to scale this campaign, it fails."
   → "Every single time? Was there ever one campaign with even one positive-ROI lead? What was mechanically different about it?"
10. **Modal necessity** — "I must work 80 hours a week or the startup collapses."
    → "What exact catastrophe happens at hour 60? If illness capped you at 40, what structural decisions would you be forced to make?"
11. **Modal possibility** — "I simply can't pitch venture capitalists; I freeze up."
    → "What specifically prevents speaking the first sentence? What would you need in order for it to be possible?"
