# Submodalities & Problem Cinematics

Source: paper §3. The nervous system codes *meaning* not by narrative content but by structural submodality parameters. Change the coding, change the response. This module is lower priority for v1 (hard to elicit in text chat) but powers the "anatomy of stuckness" diagnostics and pattern interrupts.

## Key principle: Driver Submodality

Submodalities form a coupled non-linear system. A **driver** θ_driver is the master parameter where ∂S/∂θ_driver >> ∂S/∂θ_i for all other i — shift it and the whole state collapses in tandem (e.g. shrink the catastrophic image → brightness, volume, visceral intensity all drop together). Collapse states by manipulating the driver, not by narrative analysis.

## Parameter taxonomy (abbreviated)

- **Visual**: associated (1st person) vs. dissociated (3rd person); size; distance; brightness; color vs. B&W; motion (movie vs. still); location in field; focus; 2D/3D.
- **Auditory**: internal dialogue vs. external; volume; pitch/tone; tempo; source location; clarity; mono vs. surround.
- **Kinesthetic**: location (gut, chest, throat); direction/spin; intensity 1–10; temperature; texture/density; heavy vs. light.

## Cinematics of stuck states (diagnostic signatures)

1. **Chronic anxiety** — V: high-speed looping panoramic full-color worst-case movies, 100% associated (happening NOW). A: rapid high-pitched catastrophizing "what if" dialogue. K: upward-spinning tension in solar plexus/chest.
2. **Indecisiveness / procrastination** — V: multiple competing dissociated blurry stills, low contrast, equidistant. A: loud discordant internal debate, no arbiter. K: heavy downward crushing sensation → behavioral inhibition.
3. **Compulsion** — V: massive hyper-bright close-up image dead-center, blocking peripheral context. K: forward-pulling magnetic vector.

## Transformation patterns (v2+ material)

- **Swish**: cue trigger image (large, bright, associated, full-field) + desired self-image (tiny, dim, dissociated, far corner) → in ≤500ms shrink/darken cue while exploding desired image to panoramic dominance. Repeat with break-states until trigger auto-transitions to desired image.
- **Threshold/blowout**: amplify driver submodality past processing limit (image 10,000×; 500 overlapping sound tracks; movie at 100× speed) until the synesthesia breaks and the anchor collapses.

For a text-first demo: elicit these implicitly ("when you picture the meeting, is it like you're in it or watching it? how big/close is it?"), shift association and size verbally. Full immersive protocols belong to a guided-audio version.
