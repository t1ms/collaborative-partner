# Reframing Toolkit

Source: paper §4, §6, §7.2. No behavior or event holds intrinsic meaning — meaning is an emergent function of frame.

## Content vs. context reframing

- **Content reframe** (meaning shift, context constant): X ≡ Y₁ (maladaptive) → X ≡ Y₂ (adaptive).
  - "Client scrutinizes every line item → doesn't trust us" becomes "he's under pressure to justify budget to his board and wants the proposal bulletproof to get you max allocation."
- **Context reframe** (trait constant, environment shifted): move the trait where it's optimal.
  - "Too stubborn with leadership" → "that same inflexibility protects your IP when negotiating against aggressive acquirers."

## Sleight of Mouth matrix (8 patterns)

Worked example belief: "My co-founder doesn't respect me (Y) because she challenges my technical decisions in front of the team (X)."

1. **Redefine** — "She's stress-testing the architectural integrity of the product where it matters most."
2. **Intention** — "Her goal is a codebase that scales without downtime, not diminishing you."
3. **Consequence** — "Believing debate = disrespect makes you defensive, which actually erodes the team's faith in your leadership."
4. **Chunk Down** — "Which specific argument did she raise — and was the technical critique valid?"
5. **Chunk Up** — "Isn't rigorous intellectual friction the hallmark of world-class engineering culture?"
6. **Counter-Example** — "Ever challenged a colleague's proposal precisely because you respected their intellect?"
7. **Model of the World** — "In engineering culture, open technical challenge is the highest form of collaboration."
8. **Apply to Self** — "By assuming critique means disrespect, aren't you disrespecting her dedication to the system?"

## Positive intent & ascending intent laddering

Every behavior has an underlying positive intention (I_pos) at the subsystem level; maladaptation = obsolete execution strategy (S_exec ≠ I_pos). 
- **Core Transformation Backward-Chaining:** When resistance surfaces, chain up the hierarchy of intention ("If you achieve X, what even higher outcome does that give you?") until reaching the foundational core value, then integrate it back into the outcome architecture.
- **Example:** manuscript-sabotage via trivia rabbit-holes → "What protective function is that research part serving — safe container from judgment, or novelty injection against burnout? If the manuscript were guaranteed profound and safe, what would that part need to let you write the next chapter?"

## Meta-State reframing (reflexive cognitive loops)

Human cognition is recursive: we evaluate our own primary states (e.g. *guilt about anger*, *anxiety about indecision*, *shame about fear*). Pathology often lives in the toxic secondary frame, not the primary state.
- **Protocol:** Identify the secondary evaluation ("I hate that I'm hesitant") → outframe with curiosity/acceptance ("What if hesitation is simply your brain signaling missing data?") → collapse the reflexive loop.

## Perceptual positions & psychological distancing (4)

Active cognitive mechanism: **Psychological Distancing** (downregulates autonomic arousal, freeing central executive working memory to analyze interaction dynamics without threat reactivity).

1. **1st (Self)** — associated, own direct experience/needs.
2. **2nd (Other)** — cognitive perspective-taking: counterpart's constraints, incentives, and bandwidth. "From inside their map, what makes my request look high-risk or low-priority?"
3. **3rd (Observer)** — objective camera view: watching 1st+2nd interact without affective charge.
4. **4th (Systemic)** — structural/field-level view: team norms, incentive alignment, governance loops.

Protocol for interpersonal deadlock: 1st (raw complaint) → 3rd (psychological distance / objective geometry) → 2nd (counterpart incentives) → 4th (systemic incentives) → back to 1st with a de-escalated strategy.

## Temporal manipulation: prospective hindsight

Shift user to t+10 years, crisis now a resolved footnote. Backcast: "What was the very first obvious decision that unlocked it? What were you worrying about that turned out irrelevant?"

## Pattern interrupts (§4.3) — v1: opt-in only

- Grotesque exaggeration: "Career over from one mistake? Yes — trapdoor, collapsing ceiling, national guard escorts you to the island for incompetent speakers. Before or after the audience combusts?"
- Hyper-literal spatialization: "That weight on your shoulders — what color is it? If it fell off your left shoulder, would it crack the floorboards?"
- Non-sequitur state break: "Before you continue feeling that — did you remember to breathe out of your left ear, or waiting until Tuesday?"
Mechanism: orienting response crashes the current loop, opening t_plastic for installing a Well-Formed Outcome.

## Utilization & fractionation

- **Utilization**: never argue with resistance — use it. "Your skepticism is exactly what makes this work: an uncritical mind accepts superficial answers; your skepticism ensures we reject anything that isn't airtight."
- **Fractionation**: rapid cycling between associated problem-immersion and dissociated meta-analysis to break emotional inertia.
